# Advanced Vehicles

A compendium of vehicles, siege equipment, vehicle weapons and ammunition for D&D 5e in Foundry VTT v14.

**Requirements:** Foundry VTT v14 and the D&D 5e system 6.0.6 or newer. This module contains content only, with no scripts or settings.

## Installation

In Foundry's **Add-on Modules** tab choose **Install Module** and paste this manifest URL:

```
https://github.com/Conjectural-Technologies/advanced-vehicles/releases/latest/download/module.json
```

## Features

Four compendium packs are provided. Players have Observer access and assistants have Owner access. Pack contents are binary databases, so the lists below are representative rather than exhaustive.

| Pack | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Actors** | Vehicles and vehicle components, including an airship, helms, a special helm, turrets, a harpoon gun, a light turret, shields, rams (blunt and piercing), crushing wheels, grappling jaws, raking scythes, a propulsion unit, and ray weapons (disintegration, energy and paralyzing). Also includes creature-themed siege entries such as a beholder-style vehicle with eyestalk cannons. |
| **Items** | Vehicle ammunition and components such as kegs, stones, rays, rams and upgrades. |
| **Journals** | A reference journal with an ammunition table (ballista bolts, cannonballs, harpoons, kegs and more) and a rules entry for gunpowder, including when a container ignites or explodes. |
| **Active Effects** | Reusable effects for use with vehicles. |

About 35 bundled SVG icons cover vehicle and siege parts (sails, ship wheel, siege tower, wrecking ball), weapons (harpoons, flamethrower, scythe, arrowheads), and effects like disintegrate and teleport.

The module works well alongside **Advanced Drag & Drop**, which lets you load items and creatures onto vehicles as cargo and crew.
